## Introduction

Real estate is a strong beginner niche for an AI video service because:
- agents need **constant** content
- listings and local lifestyle provide endless material
- short videos are now standard marketing

You don’t need to be a real estate expert.

You need a repeatable video system.

---

## Why real estate is a great beginner niche

Agents are in a competitive market.

They win by:
- being visible weekly
- building trust
- showing local authority

Short-form videos help with all three.

And most agents don’t have time to edit.

---

## 10 short video ideas

Use these as repeatable formats.

1) “New listing in [Area]” (3 highlights + CTA)  
2) “Open house this weekend” (time, location, why visit)  
3) “3 things buyers miss in [Area]”  
4) “Rent vs buy: one simple rule” (no hard promises)  
5) “What $[Budget] gets you in [Area]” (range-based, not exact)  
6) “The buying timeline in 30 seconds”  
7) “How to make an offer that gets accepted” (general tips)  
8) “Neighborhood spotlight: [Area]” (schools, commute, lifestyle)  
9) “Client FAQ: [Question]” (one answer)  
10) “Mistake of the week” (inspection, financing, paperwork basics)  

Each idea should become a 20–45 second video.

## A simple beginner package

Keep the offer simple:

### Starter package (recommended)

- 4 videos / month (1 per week)
- vertical format
- 1 revision round
- delivered every Monday

Upgrade path:
- 8 videos/month (2 per week)
- add repurposing (more output from one idea)

## Weekly workflow

Use a weekly routine:

1) Collect inputs (listing info, CTA, dates)  
2) Write a short script outline  
3) Produce 1–2 videos using your template  
4) Deliver and request one list of feedback
