## Quick list: the 15 best ideas

1. Start a niche blog (affiliate + email later)
2. Create a simple service offer (freelance, consulting)
3. Print-on-demand store (validate designs)
4. Sell digital templates (Notion, Canva, spreadsheets)
5. UGC creator services for brands
6. Local lead generation sites
7. Micro SaaS with a single feature
8. Newsletter + sponsorships
9. Online course (only after you get results)
10. Paid community
11. Marketplace flipping (skills-based)
12. Affiliate reviews for SaaS tools
13. Job board / directory (niche)
14. Virtual assistant agency
15. B2B outbound service (done-for-you)

## How to choose (3-minute method)

Pick based on **speed**, **skills**, and **scalability**:

- If you want the fastest first revenue: services.
- If you want the best long-term compounding: SEO content cluster.
- If you want ecommerce: start small and validate.
