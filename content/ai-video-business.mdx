## Introduction

One of the biggest misconceptions about making money with AI is that you need to build an audience.

You don’t.

In fact, the fastest way beginners are earning online right now is not YouTube, not TikTok and not automation pages.

It is selling a **simple service**.

Businesses need videos.  
Short videos, ads, social media clips, product videos and promotional content.

And they need it constantly.

Most small businesses know video works.  
They just don’t know how to create it.

That is where the opportunity is.

If you have not read the general overview first, start here:

This article shows a specific model:  
how beginners create videos using AI tools and get their first paying clients.

## Why Businesses Are Desperate For Video

Today, every platform is video-first:

- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts
- Facebook Ads
- Ecommerce product pages

But small businesses face three problems:

1. They don’t know editing  
2. They don’t want to show their face  
3. Agencies are expensive

A marketing agency may charge $800-$3000/month.

You can offer a simpler service for a fraction of that and still make good money.

You are not competing with agencies.

You are solving a problem they cannot afford.

---

## What You Actually Sell

You are not selling "AI".

You are selling:

- short form videos
- promotional ads
- product videos
- social media content
- real estate walkthrough videos
- local business ads

The client never cares which tools you use.

They care about:  
**"Can you create videos for my business every week?"**

That’s it.

---

## The Workflow (Very Important)

Your job becomes a repeatable system.

### Step 1 - Script

You generate a short marketing script using AI.  
Example: restaurant promo, fitness ad, realtor video.

### Step 2 - Voiceover

Create a human-like narration using:

[ElevenLabs](https://try.elevenlabs.io/uo7tjoejxk32)

### Step 3 - Video

Turn the script into a complete video using:

[InVideo](https://invideo.sjv.io/3JrYry)  
or  
[Pictory](https://pictory.ai?ref=pictoryavis)

### Step 4 - Optional Avatar Presenter

Some businesses want a human presenter.

You can create one using:

[HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/?sid=rewardful&via=rayzvideoai)

### Step 5 - Shorts Editing

Cut vertical clips for social media:

[CapCut](https://capcutaffiliateprogram.pxf.io/yq111D)

You now have a full production pipeline without filming anything.

## What You Charge

Beginner pricing (very realistic):

- $40 - $80 per short video
- $120 - $300 for an ad
- $300 - $800 monthly package

You only need **2-4 clients** to create real monthly income.

This is why this model works better than trying to become an influencer.

You get paid immediately.

## Where To Find Clients

You do NOT need ads.

You do NOT need a website portfolio first.

You need outreach.

Start with local businesses:

- restaurants
- gyms
- dentists
- real estate agents
- car dealerships

Open Google Maps in your city.

Look for businesses with:
- poor social media
- no videos
- old posts

These are perfect prospects.

## The First Message (Simple Script)

Send a short message:

> Hi, I created a short example promo video idea for your business.  
> I can produce social media videos like this weekly.  
> Would you like me to send you a sample?

Do not overthink it.

You are not applying for a job.  
You are offering help.
