## A 30-day beginner plan

Week 1
- pick one model and one niche
- set up the site and publish 1 page

Week 2
- publish 2 pages
- add internal links to older content

Week 3
- publish 2 pages
- add 1 commercial page (review / best / comparison)

Week 4
- publish 2 pages
- update the main “pillar” page to link to everything

## Common mistakes

- changing niche every week
- publishing random topics without internal links
- waiting for a perfect logo or design before publishing
- never updating pages after you publish them
