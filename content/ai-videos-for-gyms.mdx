## Introduction

Fitness is one of the easiest niches to sell video services to because:
- people buy with emotion and momentum
- short-form content performs well
- the topics repeat (which makes production fast)

You don’t need a complex strategy.

You need a weekly content system.

---

## Why fitness is a strong niche

Gyms and trainers need:
- new leads
- consistent visibility
- social proof

But most of them:
- film randomly
- post inconsistently
- don’t have time to edit

Your offer is a simple package:

**“I deliver 1–2 short videos/week for your gym.”**

---

## 10 short video ideas

Rotate these formats weekly:

1) exercise demo (1 movement, 1 cue)  
2) “common mistake” correction  
3) quick warm-up routine  
4) 3 tips for beginners  
5) trainer intro (why you coach)  
6) member story (social proof)  
7) class schedule highlight  
8) limited-time offer (trial week)  
9) myth vs reality (simple)  
10) FAQ answer (“how many days/week?”)  

Keep them 20–45 seconds.

---

## A simple package

Beginner package:
- 4 videos/month (1 per week)
- vertical format
- one revision round
- delivered weekly

Upgrade:
- 8 videos/month (2 per week)
- add repurposing (more shorts from one idea)
