## Introduction

Most clients don’t need “more editing”.

They need more **consistent content**.

That’s why repurposing is such a powerful service:

One idea → many videos.

This is also how you increase value without burning out.

---

## Why repurposing is the real skill

Anyone can make one video.

The winners can make:
- 10 variations
- without starting from scratch
- on a weekly schedule

Repurposing works because:
- audiences need repetition
- platforms reward consistency
- businesses don’t have time to create fresh ideas daily

---

## The 10 short angles template

Use this template to turn one topic into 10 short videos.

Start with a single idea:

Example: “How to get more bookings for a local business”

Now generate 10 angles:

1) the biggest mistake  
2) the fastest win  
3) the unpopular truth  
4) the “do this, not that”  
5) the checklist  
6) the myth  
7) the before/after story  
8) the quick tip  
9) the mini case study  
10) the FAQ answer  

Each angle becomes one short.

---

## The workflow step by step

### Step 1: pick the source

Your source can be:
- one long script
- one blog post
- one client offer + benefits
- one customer question

### Step 2: write 10 hooks

Hooks are the first 1–2 lines.

Example hook types:
- “Most people do this wrong…”
- “If you only do one thing this week…”
- “Stop doing this…”

### Step 3: outline each short

Each short should be:
- hook
- one point
- CTA

### Step 4: produce with templates

Make 1–2 templates and reuse them:
- same fonts
- same caption style
- same pacing

Templates are how you scale output.

### Step 5: deliver as a package

Bundle the shorts into a weekly delivery:
- 2 shorts/week (beginner)
- 3 shorts/week (growth)

## Caption and hook rules

Small rules that improve results:

- Put the hook as on-screen text immediately
- Keep captions short (one sentence)
- Use simple language, not jargon
- Show the CTA on screen (not only spoken)

Your goal is clarity, not creativity.

---

## Weekly delivery example

Simple schedule you can promise to clients:

- Monday: deliver 2 shorts
- Thursday: deliver 2 shorts

This creates predictable output and fewer “urgent requests”.
