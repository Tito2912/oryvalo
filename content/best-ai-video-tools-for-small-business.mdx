## Introduction

If you’re a small business (or you run a service for small businesses), AI video can feel confusing.

There are too many tools.

The mistake is building a “stack” of 12 apps you never master.

Instead, you need a small set of capabilities that produce videos consistently.

---

## The simple stack you actually need

You need 4 capabilities:

1) scripting (ideas + structure)  
2) voice (optional)  
3) video creation/editing  
4) captions + formatting  

Everything else is optional.

If you want a broader beginner list (not only video), start here:

➡️ [Best AI Tools for Beginners](/best-ai-tools-for-beginners)

## How to choose tools without overthinking

Pick tools based on your workflow.

Ask:

- Do I need text-to-video or template-based editing?
- Do I need voice, or will captions carry the message?
- Do I need repurposing (1 idea → many shorts)?
- Do I need client delivery and revisions?

Then pick one tool per job.

Do not pick tools because they are trending.

Pick tools because they solve a step in your weekly SOP.

## A beginner weekly setup

Here’s a simple weekly system:

1) pick one topic (offer, FAQ, tip)  
2) write one short script  
3) produce 2 shorts from it  
4) deliver on the same day every week  

Consistency creates results.

Tools help.

But workflow is what makes it real.
