## Introduction

Restaurants live and die by attention.

And attention today is video.

The good news:

Restaurants don’t need cinematic edits.

They need a simple system that produces consistent Reels and Shorts every week.

That’s why restaurants are a great niche for an AI video service.

---

## Why restaurants buy content

Most restaurant owners want:
- more reservations
- more walk-ins
- better visibility on Instagram/TikTok/Google

But they don’t have time to edit daily.

Your offer is not “AI”.

Your offer is:

**“I deliver 1–2 short videos/week for your restaurant.”**

---

## 10 Reels ideas you can repeat

Pick 3–5 formats and rotate weekly.

1) “Today’s special” (price optional)  
2) “Behind the scenes” (kitchen prep montage)  
3) “3 best dishes to try”  
4) “Customer favorite” (top-selling item)  
5) “How it’s made” (quick process)  
6) “Before/after plating”  
7) “Chef tip of the week”  
8) “New menu item launch”  
9) “Review highlight” (social proof)  
10) “Event night” (live music, happy hour, theme night)  

Keep them short: 20–45 seconds.

---

## A simple package to sell

Start with a monthly package:

- 4 videos/month (1 per week)
- vertical format
- one revision round
- delivered weekly

Upgrade path:
- 8 videos/month (2 per week)
- add repurposing (more output from one idea)
