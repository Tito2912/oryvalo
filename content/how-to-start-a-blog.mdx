## Why blogging still works
Blogs compound over time when pages match search intent and are internally linked.

## The 7-step system
1. Pick one niche.
2. Build a starter cluster.
3. Publish the first 10 pages.
4. Interlink every page.
5. Update based on GSC data.
6. Add commercial pages.
7. Repeat weekly.
