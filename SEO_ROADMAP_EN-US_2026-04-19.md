# SEO Roadmap (EN/US) — oryvalo.com (2026-04-19)

This roadmap is designed for a **new domain** with low authority to get:
1) **more impressions** (long-tail coverage)
2) first **clicks**
3) a path to **affiliate revenue**

It assumes you keep the site in **English** and target **US intent**.

Implementation note (repo): the AI video cluster pages + `/ai-video` hub were added on **April 19, 2026**. The next step is deployment + GSC indexing.

---

## 0) The decision that changes everything (pick ONE cluster)

Right now, the site is too broad for its authority level.

Recommended single cluster for the next 60–90 days:

**Cluster: “AI video creation service” (for small businesses)**

Why:
- It matches the existing content (`/ai-video-business`, `/make-money-with-ai`).
- It creates natural “money pages” (tools, comparisons, workflows).
- It generates dozens of low-competition long-tail angles (industries, packages, scripts, workflows).

Secondary clusters can wait.

---

## 1) Success metrics (what “working” looks like)

Because the site is early, measure progress in **impressions** first.

Targets (realistic for a fresh site):
- Day 30: **300–1,000 impressions / 28 days**
- Day 60: **1,000–3,000 impressions / 28 days**
- Day 90: **3,000–10,000 impressions / 28 days**

Clicks will lag; that’s normal.

---

## 2) 30-day publishing plan (fast but quality-focused)

Goal: publish **12 pages** (1 hub + 1 updated pillar + 10 supports).

### Week 1 — Fix structure + publish 2 supports
- Create a dedicated hub page: `/ai-video` (links all video pages).
- Update the pillar `/ai-video-business` to link to the new supports (“Next steps” + inline links).
- Publish 2 support articles.

### Week 2 — Publish 3 supports
- 1 “business model” page
- 1 “workflow” page
- 1 “industry” page

### Week 3 — Publish 3 supports
- 1 “templates/scripts” page (linkable asset)
- 2 “industry” pages

### Week 4 — Publish 2 supports + optimize
- Publish 2 supports (based on what gets impressions first).
- Optimize titles/intro for queries that appear in GSC.
- Add internal links from older pages to the new cluster (every relevant page should link to `/ai-video`).

---

## 3) The 10 support pages to create (recommended)

All pages should be:
- long-tail (clear intent)
- very actionable (templates, steps, examples)
- internally linked back to `/ai-video-business` + `/make-money-with-ai`

### Business model (high intent)
1) **AI video service pricing** (packages + positioning + when to raise prices)
2) **How to get clients for an AI video service** (lead sources + outreach scripts)
3) **How to build an AI video portfolio (without clients)** (3 samples + structure)

### Workflow (mid intent, easy to rank)
4) **AI video workflow: script → voice → edit → delivery** (checklists, SOP)
5) **How to turn 1 video into 10 shorts** (repurposing process)

### Industry pages (low competition, scalable)
6) **AI videos for real estate agents** (10 ideas + package)
7) **AI videos for restaurants** (10 ideas + package)
8) **AI videos for gyms / personal trainers** (10 ideas + package)

### Tool decision pages (affiliate-friendly without being spammy)
9) **Best AI video tools for small businesses (simple stack)** (criteria, “best for X”)
10) **InVideo vs Pictory (beginner workflow comparison)** (use cases, tradeoffs)

---

## 4) Internal linking rules (non-negotiable)

For the AI video cluster:
- Every support page links to:
  - `/ai-video-business`
  - `/make-money-with-ai`
  - `/ai-tools`
- The pillar (`/ai-video-business`) links to every support page.
- The hub (`/ai-video`) links to pillar + all supports.

This is how you “teach” Google the site structure.

---

## 5) Link building (minimum viable, month 1)

Don’t overthink it. Month 1 goal: **10 new referring domains**.

Easy wins:
- 3–5 profiles (relevant creator profiles / directories)
- 2–3 guest posts (small blogs, newsletters)
- 2–3 tool partner/community pages (where allowed)

Anchor rule: mostly branded (“Oryvalo”), not exact-match.

---

## 6) GSC weekly loop (30 minutes / week)

Every week:
1) **Performance → Queries**: find anything with impressions + position 11–30.
2) Update the matching page:
   - improve title/intro alignment with the query
   - add the missing section (H2)
   - add 2 internal links to related cluster pages

This is how a new site climbs.
